Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short

We collect only what we need to publish the news, run subscriptions and courses, answer your messages, consider job applications and keep the site secure. We never see or store your full card details, we do not sell your personal data, and you can ask us to access, correct or erase it at any time.

1.Who we are and how to reach us

Digital Truth News Network (https://amal-davis.github.io/dtn-client-preview) is run by Demo Media Private Limited, Demo Building, MG Road, Kochi, Kerala 682001, India. For this policy, we are the “Data Fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”): we decide why and how your personal data is processed.

Questions, requests and complaints about your personal data go to our Grievance Officer at desk@example.com. Full contact details are at the end of this page.

2.What this policy covers

This policy covers personal data we process through the site, our newsletter, subscriptions, courses, the contact and advertising enquiry forms and job applications, and the accounts of our reporters and staff. It does not cover other sites or services that we link to or embed, which have their own policies.

3.What we collect and why

The table lists each kind of personal data, with examples, why we use it and where it comes from. We do not ask for more than each purpose needs.

4.How we use your data and our lawful basis

Under the DPDP Act we process personal data either with your consent or for a “legitimate use” that the Act allows.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for purposes you would not reasonably expect.

5.Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. We use as few as we can.

You can control cookies:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working.
  • Turn off personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

6.Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the site, under contracts that limit them to our instructions, and only as much as each one needs.

We may also disclose personal data when the law requires it, for example to comply with a court order or a lawful request from a government agency, to protect the rights and safety of our readers, staff or others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business (in which case this policy continues to protect it).

7.Data stored outside India

Our servers are in India. Some providers, such as Google, Cloudflare and Sentry, may process data in other countries. We use them only where the DPDP Act permits such transfers, and we choose providers that protect data to a high standard.

9.How we protect your data

We use reasonable security measures, including:

  • All traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes; card details never reach our servers.
  • IP addresses used for spam protection and view counts are turned into one-way codes.
  • CVs are kept in a private storage area that is not reachable from the website; only our administrators can open them, and notification emails never include them.
  • Access to the editing system is limited to staff who need it, with separate roles for reporters, editors and administrators.
  • Servers are kept updated, protected by a firewall and backed up daily.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10.Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correction and completion: ask us to correct inaccurate data or complete missing data.
  • Erasure: ask us to erase your data when it is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent you gave (for example to the newsletter) as easily as you gave it. This does not affect anything we did before.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate: name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to do so.

To use any of these rights, email desk@example.com from the email address linked to your data (or tell us how to verify you). We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and respond within one month at the latest; the law allows up to 90 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

11.Children

The site is a general news service and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Where a child's data is needed (for example for a course), we will first obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, as the DPDP Act requires, and we will not track children or show them targeted advertising. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email desk@example.com and we will delete it.

12.Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you on the site or by email, and ask for fresh consent where the law requires it.

13.Contact

Privacy questions and requests: desk@example.com. Other questions: desk@example.com or our Contact page.

Our Grievance Officer handles complaints about our content, subscriptions, payments and personal data.
