Refund Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short

You can cancel a subscription at any time; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again. We don't refund partly used periods, but we always refund mistakes such as duplicate charges, failed-payment debits and charges after you cancelled.

1.What this policy covers

This policy covers payments to Demo Media Private Limited for Digital Truth News Network digital subscriptions, paid courses and live events. Everything we sell is digital and delivered online, so there is nothing to ship or return.

How plans, billing and auto-renewal work is explained in our Subscription Policy.

2.The free plan

Our free plan costs nothing. No payment is taken, so no refund applies.

3.Cancelling a paid subscription

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops all future renewals. Your access continues until the end of the billing period you have already paid for, and then your account moves to the free plan.

To cancel, use your account page (when available) or email desk@example.com from your registered email address. We confirm every cancellation by email.

4.When we refund

We do not refund the unused part of a billing period after you cancel. We do give a full refund of the affected charge in these cases:

  • you were charged twice for the same period (duplicate charge);
  • money was debited but the payment failed and your plan was not activated (most banks reverse these automatically; if yours has not, we will help);
  • you were charged after we had confirmed your cancellation;
  • a renewal was charged without the pre-debit notification that RBI rules require;
  • we were unable to provide the plan you paid for (for example, we discontinued it), in which case we refund the unused part of the period.

If we suspend or close an account because of a serious breach of our Terms and Conditions (for example sharing paid content publicly), no refund is due for the rest of the period.

5.How to ask for a refund

Email desk@example.com within 30 days of the charge, from your registered email address, with:

  • your registered email address or mobile number;
  • the transaction ID or payment reference;
  • the date and amount of the charge;
  • the reason for the request.

6.How long refunds take

We review your request and reply with our decision within 7 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. The time for the money to reach you depends on the payment gateway and your bank; it typically takes 5 to 7 working days after approval.

7.Paid courses

This section applies when paid courses are available. You can ask for a full refund of a course within 7 days of buying it, as long as you have completed less than 20% of its lessons. After that, or once you have downloaded a certificate, course fees are not refundable. Courses included in a subscription plan follow the subscription rules above.

8.Live events and webinars

If we cancel a paid live class, event or webinar, you get a full refund. If we reschedule it, you can join on the new date or ask for a full refund. Fees are not refunded if you are unable to attend an event that goes ahead as scheduled; where we offer a recording, you will be able to watch it.

9.Chargebacks

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us first: we can usually fix it faster than a bank dispute. If a chargeback is raised, we may pause access to the paid plan while it is reviewed. If it is decided in your favour, the refund comes through your bank and we will not charge you again for that period.

10.Contact and complaints

Refund questions: desk@example.com. If you are not happy with how we handled a request, contact our Grievance Officer at desk@example.com. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within one month at the latest, as set out in our Terms and Conditions.

Questions or complaints?

Our Grievance Officer handles complaints about our content, subscriptions, payments and personal data.
