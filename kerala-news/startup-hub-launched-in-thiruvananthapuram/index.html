Startup hub launched in Thiruvananthapuram

Officials said the work would be completed in phases so that residents face as little disruption as possible

AM
Anjali MenonSenior Correspondent
Published 9 Nov 2026, 10:01 pm1 min read

Officials said the work would be completed in phases so that residents face as little disruption as possible. A detailed timeline will be published on the district portal next week.

Local representatives welcomed the decision and said it had been a long-standing demand. Traders in the area asked for clear diversion signs during the work.

The district administration has set up a help desk for complaints. Updates will be shared through the official social media accounts.
