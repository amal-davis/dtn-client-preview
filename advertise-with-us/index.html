Advertise with us

Reach Kerala's readers in Malayalam and English

Put your brand next to trusted local, national and world news, read every day on phones and desktops. Tell us what you want to achieve and we will suggest the right formats.

Enquire now

Our audience

Figures to be confirmed (shown only outside production): Monthly readers, Monthly page views, Newsletter subscribers, Social media followers

Why advertise with us

  • A bilingual audience

    We publish in Malayalam and English, so one campaign can speak to readers in the language they prefer.

  • Kerala and Malayalis abroad

    Our focus is Kerala's districts, read across the state and, we expect, by Malayalis living elsewhere in India and in the Gulf. Ask us for the latest reader locations.

  • A brand-safe environment

    Your ads appear beside edited journalism that follows our published Editorial Policy, never beside unmoderated content.

  • Mobile-first readers

    The site is built for phones first: fast pages, ads that load without pushing the story around.

  • Flexible formats

    Display banners, category takeovers, sponsored stories and newsletter sponsorship, alone or as a package.

  • Transparent reporting

    We agree the measures with you before the campaign starts and report against them when it ends.

Ad formats

Display sizes are the ones our pages are built around. Send creatives at these sizes (and the mobile size where given) so they fit without cropping.

  • Homepage banner

    A wide banner between the sections of the homepage, the most-visited page of the site.

    Desktop 970 × 250, mobile 300 × 250

  • Sidebar

    Beside the Most Read and Trending lists on article and section pages, on desktop.

    300 × 600 (300 × 250 also fits)

  • In-article

    Inside the story, after the third and seventh paragraphs of longer articles.

    Desktop 728 × 90, mobile 300 × 250

  • Category pages

    Within the story lists of a section such as Keralam, Sports or Business: reach readers by topic.

    Desktop 728 × 90, mobile 300 × 250

  • On request

    Sponsored story

    An article about your organisation or campaign, written or approved to our editorial standards and always labelled “Sponsored”.

  • On request

    Newsletter sponsorship

    A clearly marked sponsor message in our email newsletter.

Sponsored content rules

  • Every sponsored story, video or newsletter message is labelled Sponsored, on the page and wherever it is shared.
  • Sponsored stories are written or approved under our editorial standards: no false claims, no misleading headlines, no attacks on competitors.
  • Sponsored content is never made to look like our news reporting, and advertisers have no say over our news coverage.
  • We may decline any advertisement or sponsored content that breaks the law or our policies.

Read the full rules in our Editorial Policy.

How it works

  1. 1

    Step 1: Enquire

    Tell us about your brand, goals, budget and dates with the form below.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Proposal

    We reply with suggested formats, placements, timing and a quote.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Creative and approval

    You send the creatives (or we prepare a sponsored story with you). We check them against our ad and editorial policies before anything goes live.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Campaign live

    Your campaign runs on the agreed pages and dates.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Report

    At the end you receive a report against the measures we agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum spend?

It depends on the formats and how long the campaign runs. Tell us your budget in the form and we will suggest what fits it.

Can I target a section or a language?

Yes. Display ads can run on chosen sections (for example Keralam, Sports or Business) and we can tell you which placements reach Malayalam or English readers. Ask us what is available for your dates.

What creative specifications do you accept?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF) or HTML5 at the sizes listed under Ad formats. Keep files light so pages stay fast; we will confirm the exact file limits in the proposal.

What are the payment terms?

Payment terms are set out in each proposal and agreed before the campaign starts. Prices are subject to GST where applicable.

Do you approve ads before they go live?

Yes. Every ad and sponsored story is checked before it goes live. We do not run ads that are misleading, unlawful or that imitate news reporting (see our Editorial Policy).

Will I get a report?

Yes. We agree what we will measure before the campaign starts and send a report when it ends.
